Privacy policy for markdown · Updated 21 September 2026

Document handling

markdown edits documents on your device. The app does not send your document text to Charles Majola or to an app-operated server. It has no account system, advertising, tracking or analytics.

Files and sharing

On iPhone and iPad, your documents are saved to the location you choose in Files. iCloud Drive or another Files provider may store and sync documents under that provider’s privacy terms. Sharing a document uses the system share sheet and only happens when you choose it.

Images and links

The iOS editor blocks remote images and does not open document links. The Mac editor may load remote images referenced in a document; the image host then receives a normal network request. Opening an external link uses your browser.

Data collection

markdown does not collect personal information, document contents, identifiers, usage data or location. No camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library permissions are requested. Apple may provide platform diagnostics according to your device settings.

This website and support

This static website has no accounts or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information, such as IP address and request path, to deliver pages. If you open a GitHub support issue, the information you choose to post is handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not include private documents or credentials.

Contact

Developer: Charles Majola. For privacy questions, open a support issue or visit the developer profile.
